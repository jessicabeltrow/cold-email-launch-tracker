THE PATH TO LAUNCH

Five clear stages
1

Run the lead-data pilot

Scrape window cleaners across about 10 nearby Romford postcode districts. Filter, find emails, verify and dedupe — nothing enters Instantly yet.

Ready now
2

Measure what the pilot really produces

Track listings, duplicates, businesses with websites, email-found rate, verification pass rate and cost per verified contact.

After pilot
3

Set up fresh domains and inboxes

Buy 9 new sending domains, create 27 Google inboxes, check DNS stays with Spaceship, and add the inboxes to Instantly.

Needs approval
4

Warm the inboxes

Warm gradually for around four weeks. Keep watching authentication and inbox health while the lead pool grows.

Blocked by setup
5

Launch only when both sides are ready

Use verified leads only, then start carefully at 20 cold emails per inbox per day. Campaign details can be decided nearer the time.

Later

What “ready to send” means

  • Fresh domains have finished warm-up
  • DNS, MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC are all correct
  • The pilot has given us real lead-yield numbers
  • We have a healthy reserve of verified contacts
